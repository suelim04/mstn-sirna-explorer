MSTN siRNA: research brief

This computational study defines an MSTN siRNA construction starting point and proposed RNA–linker–peptide identities for experimental review. It does not establish increased muscle mass, strength or therapeutic efficacy.

Selected sequence

Human transcript ENST00000260950.5; [455,474), zero-based and half-open. Both complete strands are shown 5′→3′; the terminal UU is non-templated.

  • Guide: GUAGCGUGAUAAUCGUCAUUU
  • Passenger: AUGACGAUUAUCACGCUACUU

Guide and passenger have 2,711 and 4,164 canonical seed transcript-site occurrences, respectively: 6,875 combined. The selected record has no recorded other-gene full-core hit within two ungapped substitutions and no recorded GDF11 canonical seed site within the assessed 3′UTRs. These are bounded matching results, not measured selectivity.

A passenger-only count preference would choose a different window. Neither pooled counts nor zero bounded findings establish equal strand loading, actual suppression or safety.

Experimental decisions remaining

Review identity and synthesis feasibility, qualify endogenous MSTN in a suitable model, specify chemistry-matched and delivery controls, and measure MSTN RNA, the intended myostatin protein form, viability and GDF11. Replication and sampling require a predefined plan. These readouts do not establish transcriptome-wide specificity.

The project demonstrates computational sequence analysis, strand-aware molecular representation, bounded matching, stereochemistry bookkeeping, structural diagnostics and evidence-based interpretation. It does not demonstrate hands-on synthesis or assay execution.
