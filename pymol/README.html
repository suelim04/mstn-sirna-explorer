Annotated PyMOL views — BCY17868_L6_M0

These images were ray-rendered with actual open-source PyMOL 3.1.0 from the existing standalone model. They depict one generated RNA–linker–peptide conformation, not the MSTN protein, a receptor complex, or an experimentally determined structure. Rendering does not add biological validation.

Open and inspect

  • Backbone image: RNA backbone/base rings; the 14-residue peptide is displayed as a loop trace with scaffold connections. No peptide secondary structure was inferred or assigned.
  • Solid image: heavy-atom spheres, showing approximate occupied space at the radii below. This is not a solvent-accessible surface.
  • Attachment image: passenger U21 O3′, phosphate junction, L6 connector and peptide/scaffold close-up.
  • PyMOL session: open in PyMOL and choose the saved backbone, solid or attachment scene; rotate and zoom normally. Annotation leader lines are graphical callouts, not chemical bonds. Their layout is intended for the saved views; hide objects beginning annotation_ for unobstructed rotation.

The browser displays the three fixed images. Select Interactive atoms for the separate browser atom viewer. The browser viewer and PyMOL session use the same source conformation.

What is annotated

The blue guide and cyan passenger each have 21 RNA nucleotides: a 19-base paired core plus two unpaired 3′ UU residues. Violet denotes the connector/junction; gold denotes the BCY17868 peptide and TATB scaffold. There are three cysteine–scaffold thioether connections, not disulfide bonds. The peptide is not head-to-tail cyclized.

The attachment follows source atom maps 10427 (passenger U21 O3′) → 39901 (junction P) → 30021 (connector O). The displayed connector includes the C6/carbamate/clicked-BCN route specified by the saved chemical identity. M0 denotes the proposed defined stereochemical identity; it is not a measured product abundance.

This figure communicates the specified molecular components, their connectivity and one generated spatial arrangement. Separate import checks establish faithful rendering of the saved graph. Neither the appearance nor the checks establish a solution conformation, binding affinity, receptor engagement, uptake, endosomal escape, RNA loading, knockdown, off-target suppression or safety. No protein or receptor is present. Rendering uses the existing coordinates without docking or further coordinate construction.

The saved construction findings remain zero overlaps above 0.6 Å and 17 above the stricter 0.4 Å threshold. These are construction checks, not physical validation. The standalone conformation does not repair or replace the failed receptor-bound comparison.

Coordinate fidelity and software

Images were rendered with open-source PyMOL 3.1.0. The saved session preserves the 1,027 heavy atoms, 1,137 bonds and formal charges (net −43). Full chemical authority remains in the source graph and model JSON. PyMOL coordinate rounding has maximum observed per-axis displacement approximately 1.84 × 10⁻⁶ Å, below the 10⁻⁵ Å import tolerance; the PSE is not byte-identical to full-precision coordinates.

The solid view uses radii C 1.70, N 1.60, O 1.55, P 1.95 and S 1.80 Å. Hydrogens, counterions and solvent are absent. Cartoon traces, labels and leader lines are display objects, not new chemical bonds. No peptide secondary structure was inferred.
