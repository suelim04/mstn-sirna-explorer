Agreement requires the same point-estimate sign and the same zero-exclusion status. A difference in either is disagreement; neither analysis is selected after seeing results.

What the sensitivity analysis showed: the UBC block mean is 0.066165, compared with the 14A estimate of 0.075237. The 14B estimate rises to 0.091414 when equal-block weighting cuts UBC representation from 18/30 to 1/12. The analyses agree under the frozen definition, but the direction of this shift indicates that the association is weaker within the UBC family than across the singleton blocks. This is a descriptive observation about panel composition, not a subgroup test or a significance claim about UBC; the primary weighting is unchanged.

For 14A only, δ = 0.050 native units (9.16% of the 0.546 control window): exceeds δ. Labels use unrounded endpoints. This is an operational detectability choice, not a biological or clinical threshold. At the upper endpoint exactly equal to δ, strictly exceeding δ is excluded; the frozen 'bounded below δ' label includes this equality boundary.

14B has no δ comparison or MDE annotation. δ is calibrated to the 14A estimand; 14B changes the UBC weight from 18/30 to 1/12, so the two estimates target different weighted quantities. No σ_B planning value was specified. Comparing coefficients for different variance parameters establishes no power difference.

The 14A interval assumes independent, approximately iid normal gene contrasts. The family-biased panel leaves that assumption unverified; this is not a dependence-adjusted interval or homology-robust significance claim. The 14B grouping is a sensitivity convention, not proof that 12 blocks are independent. Neither interval establishes generalization to unrelated families.

Input, methods and retained records

All 2,431 original reagent-level averaged rows are retained: 1,511 PASS and 920 FAIL; all 30 genes have at least two distinct siRNAs per stratum; the minimum minority count is 12. Zero records or genes were excluded. The two Mmp7 inserts remain one gene. The historical 2,182/249 source split is metadata only; source averages are not treated as raw replicates.

Rules apply only to the original 19-base RNA core: 6–11 G/C bases, no homopolymer ≥4, and no perfect dinucleotide tract ≥8. Conservation, isoform, variant, expression, off-target and terminal-stability features are excluded. Published full guides and variable deoxy overhangs remain unchanged.

The representation check found 174/2,431 classification changes using percentage-equivalent full-21-mer bounds (7–12 G/C): 129 pass→fail and 45 fail→pass. That is why the core-only representation matters. The 338/233/364 counterfactual diagnostics were not evaluated against outcomes here. No GC matching, tuning, model, subgroup search or alternate threshold search was performed; no information-beyond-GC claim is made.

Rule-development overlap with this dataset is not established. This is a retrospective evaluation, not an independent validation. Inherited source QC, reagent averaging and activity normalization are documented in input characterization and source audit.

Homology and dependence

The source review identifies 19 author-flagged E2 labels, including FLJ11011 = UBE2W; the conventional UBC subset contains 18. Those 18 form the frozen UBC block; Ufc1 is a singleton with broader UBC-like relationships retained as a caveat. P2X contains Hs:P2RX3 and Rn:P2rx2; all other exact memberships are in the frozen plan and evaluation.json.

The existing relatedness audit covers all 435 label pairs and 464 insert pairs. Highlighted related sets include UBE2L3/UBE2L6, UBE2D3/UBE2E3 and human P2RX3/rat P2rx2. Thirty source identities do not establish 30 independent statistical groups; sequence identity provides no unique effective G. Original source labels, including the rat TCAP accession-history caveat, remain unchanged.

The preflight's dependence scenarios assume equal marginal variances, one 18-member UBC group with common correlation ρ, and zero other covariance: G_eff = 900/(30 + 306ρ). These are assumptions, not estimated correlations or replacement degrees of freedom. The saved scenarios are carried forward unchanged:

This is a conditional sampling-noise benchmark, not an absolute floor derived from counts or a hard lower bound on realized dispersion. Rows at σ_D=0.02–0.04 are optimistic relative to it.
