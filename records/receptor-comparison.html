Receptor-bound reconstruction: result and limits

The intended comparison placed linker and peptide-handle designs against a published TfR1-bound related peptide as a fixed reference. The template was PDB 9GH7, containing BCY15466. It is not an experimental pose of the proposed conjugates. Biological assembly 1 retained two receptor copies, glycans and calcium.

Shared peptide coordinates were retained and altered side chains were rebuilt. The resulting fixed peptide core contained 107 heavy atoms: 97 source-coordinate atoms and 10 rebuilt atoms. Before linker enumeration, the core was assessed using the declared heavy-atom overlap diagnostic.

Geometric criterion and its source

Overlap = radius(i) + radius(j) − interatomic distance, in angstroms. The primary numerical cutoff was 0.6 Å, with 0.4 and 0.8 Å sensitivities. This matches the default numerical cutoff documented for ChimeraX clashes, providing a documented convention for applying the same close-contact screen across designs.

The implementation was not the ChimeraX default algorithm: it used RDKit 2026.03.6 PeriodicTable.GetRvdw element radii and zero hydrogen-bond allowance. ChimeraX's documented hydrogen-bond allowance is different. The RDKit source table attributes the radii to BODR v10.1; this is not a Bondi-only or ChimeraX atom-type table.

Calcium uses the element-vdW convention, not an ionic radius or coordination-energy model. Atom pairs within three covalent bonds were excluded. The reference comparison used inclusive boundaries with a 10⁻¹⁰ Å numerical distance tolerance. Rebuilt atoms could not inherit a reference-overlap exemption.

Recorded result

A rebuilt serine oxygen and a retained aspartate oxygen, eleven bonds apart, were 2.126546 Å apart. With oxygen radii of 1.55 Å each, their overlap was 0.973454 Å. This failed all three thresholds for both handles. Both atoms were fixed, so changing linker torsions could not remove this pair's overlap.

No fixed-core overlaps with external obstacles were flagged; the complete mobile conjugate was not assessed. The failure is internal to this reference-based reconstructed peptide. It does not establish that the molecule cannot be synthesised, bind TfR1 or enter cells.

The standalone illustration uses a separate construction method and does not repair this failed comparison. Further comparative work needs a justified new construction method or a demonstrated implementation correction. No radii or thresholds were adjusted to clear this result.
