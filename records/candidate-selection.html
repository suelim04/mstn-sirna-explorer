Tradeoff and bounded coverage

The chosen sequence also minimizes guide-only seed counts among the five qualified candidates. Passenger-only counts instead favor window [676,695), with 3,093 sites versus 4,164 for the chosen window. Equal count aggregation is a declared preference; it does not assert equal strand activity or equal effects of seed classes.

GDF11 coverage is demonstrated for ENST00000257868.10, UTR [1419,8801), and ENST00000546799.1, UTR [1142,1258). The latter has incomplete start/CDS annotation while its annotated 3′UTR is assessed. These records have no excluded noncanonical windows; this does not establish coverage of absent references or unmodeled biological contexts. INHBA is retained separately and never gates selection.

Six-mer/noncanonical interactions, expression, tissue exposure, variants, chemistry effects and experimental activity remain unassessed. The chosen guide/passenger sequences and all six class counts for each of the 176 records are available in data/all_176_candidates.tsv.
