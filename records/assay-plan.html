Proposed experimental handoff

Proposed work; no synthesis or experiment has been performed. The computational outputs define a reviewable starting point, not a laboratory-ready or validated reagent.

Preparation and controls

Review the complete reagent identity, synthesis and purification feasibility, analytical characterization and formulation. Qualify a model with measurable endogenous MSTN and distinguish cellular uptake from productive cytosolic delivery.

Use untreated and delivery-only controls, chemistry-matched negative siRNAs, an independently targeted candidate with a different site and seed, and a qualified functional delivery/process control. Define biological replication, sampling schedule and interpretation criteria before experiments. The computational analysis supplies no dose or experimental acceptance threshold.

MSTN-only knockdown cannot validate off-target-match axes. Eight measurements across correlated panel features cannot identify per-axis effects. Each feature describes a design intent; it is not an experimentally resolved mechanism.

Interpreting possible outcomes

Observed patternInterpretation and next check
RNA decreases and viability decreasesResolve toxicity, delivery and measurement explanations before assigning target-specific activity
RNA decreases but protein does notConfirm RNA, examine timing and compartment, specify protein form and assay cross-reactivity; report no detected protein change under measured conditions
No planned readout changesCheck endogenous expression, assay qualification, reagent identity and functional delivery; do not infer universal sequence inactivity
RNA and qualified protein/readouts change with acceptable viabilityCompare independent candidates and consider a separately qualified downstream mechanism experiment; the pattern does not prove specificity

A conditional mature-myostatin add-back would require a pre-established dose-response and a predefined downstream readout. It would test recovery of that downstream response, not reversal of RNA depletion or siRNA specificity.

For candidates in the conserved 3′UTR block [2232,2254), disturbance of endogenous regulation is a separate hypothesis. Conservation alone does not establish regulatory function. A discriminating control remains undefined; a downstream ligand add-back would not resolve that distinction by itself.

Sequence counts, geometric conformity and an attractive rendering do not establish potency, binding, productive delivery, safety or clinical suitability.
