Chemical identity and provenance

Twelve proposed identities combine two peptide handles (BCY17868 and BCY17869), three alkyl spacer lengths (L4, L6, L8), and two mapping-derived stereochemical forms (M0, M1). All connections are specified before coordinate generation. No measured isomer ratio, product abundance or molecular ranking is assigned.

The exact published RNA–linker support could not be established from the available sources. Consequently, these are new component-based proposed conjugates, not recovered exact copies of a published conjugate. Source peptide identities, a documented BCN component and an RNA–phosphate/alkyl/carbamate connection informed the explicit new design. L4 and L8 are specified variants of the L6 spacer.

Connectivity

The guide remains a separate covalent component. Passenger U21 O3′ connects through phosphate, an alkyl spacer, carbamate and a clicked BCN junction to the peptide/scaffold. Three cysteine–TATB thioether bonds cyclise the peptide; these are not disulfides or head-to-tail cyclization. Source stereochemistry and terminal amides are explicit. BCY17868 carries the C-terminal Lys(N₃) handle; BCY17869 uses N-terminal AzPro.

RNA is an unmodified computational architecture with 19 paired bases plus a 3′ UU overhang on each strand. Net formal charge and histidine tautomer are declared representation conventions, not measured solution populations. No counterions or solvent are represented.
