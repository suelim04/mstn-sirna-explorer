Standalone 3D model: BCY17868_L6_M0

One complete, generated conformation is available in model.sdf and model.json. It preserves the exact chemical-identity chemical graph and every coordinate of the existing G0 A-RNA duplex. The model passes the declared graph, stereochemistry, local geometry and 0.6 Å heavy-atom-overlap construction checks. It is a standalone illustration for inspection, not a measured structure, stable solution conformation, receptor-bound pose, or evidence of delivery or efficacy.

BCY17868_L6_M0 was chosen as a fully specified example for illustration. It was not selected as the best molecule. This construction does not repair or rerun the failed receptor-bound comparison receptor-bound comparison.

How the coordinates were constructed

RDKit 2026.03.6 generated a constrained terminal-uridine plus peptide/linker/junction fragment with ETKDGv3. Every terminal-uridine heavy atom was anchored to the saved RNA coordinates. A temporary H cap at the fragment boundary restored valence during fragment construction; that cap was not exported. NumPy 2.5.3 assembled the generated atoms with the complete unchanged RNA duplex.

ETKDG uses distance bounds, empirical torsion preferences, macrocycle handling and internal numerical geometry refinement. It is not a minimization-free procedure. No UFF or MMFF molecular-mechanics optimization, molecular dynamics, energy ranking, receptor-guided modification or docking was performed.

The first construction method passed graph, stereochemistry and local geometry but allowed the independently generated appendage to intersect the rest of the RNA. Its 0.6 Å diagnostic had 258 flags: 253 between RNA and appended atoms, and 5 within the appendage. Those outputs are not the delivered conformation.

Method v2 was recorded before its execution. It retained the same exact graph, RNA anchors, embedding settings, geometry intervals, radii and overlap threshold. It used a finite list of 64 seeds, each limited to one embedding attempt and a 30-second timeout. Fragments with a stereochemical, local-geometry or internal 0.6 Å overlap failure were rejected. Eligible fragments were placed using rigid rotations of the complete appendage about C3′–O3′ and O3′–P. Each angle had the fixed ordered values 0, 30, …, 330 degrees; the nested order was specified in advance. These rotations preserve appendage internal distances and junction bond lengths and angles. They never move an RNA atom.

The first passing conformation was accepted; no lowest-energy or best-overlap conformation was sought. Success occurred on seed 20672711, the 53rd listed seed. Of the 53 attempts, 13 failed to embed and 40 produced coordinates; 8 passed the local geometry checks. The accepted fragment used torsions [0°, 120°], the fifth placement tested for that fragment. Every attempted seed, failure reason and generated pre-placement coordinate set is retained. No third construction method was needed or run.

Steric diagnostic and its limits

Distances were checked for every heavy-atom pair whose shortest covalent graph distance exceeds three bonds, including pairs across the two disconnected components. Radii are the existing project diagnostic convention: C 1.70, N 1.60, O 1.55, P 1.95 and S 1.80 Å. An overlap is the sum of radii minus distance. No hydrogen-bond allowance or radius change was introduced.
