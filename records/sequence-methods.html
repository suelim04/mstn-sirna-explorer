Sequence design and bounded matching

Human MSTN transcript ENST00000260950.5, GENCODE 49, GRCh38.p14, contains 2,819 nucleotides. Every 19-nucleotide window was enumerated in one-nucleotide increments across coding and untranslated sequence: 2,801 windows. Transcript coordinates are zero-based, half-open.

Strand architecture

For a transcript-sense target T, the passenger paired core is T and the guide core is its RNA reverse complement. A non-templated 3′ UU is appended to each core, yielding two 21-nucleotide strands. Core mapping, composition and conservation exclude these overhangs. Seed queries use positions 2–8 of each explicitly labelled strand.

Orthologous-site assignment

The human and mouse transcripts were aligned; the candidate site was projected through that alignment. Conservation required a unique, consecutive mouse assignment for all nineteen positions. Multiple equal-optimal assignments remained unresolved. A uniquely assigned gap or noncontiguous site failed the ungapped conservation rule. Exact sequence conservation does not establish delivery or activity in mice.

Full-core and seed searches

Both strands were searched independently. Full-core searches covered 533,740 pinned mature transcripts with zero, one or two ungapped substitutions. G–U counted as a substitution. Non-ACGU reference bases were not wildcards. Intended MSTN matches remained distinguishable from other-gene findings.

Canonical seed searches covered 220,484 transcripts with resolved annotated 3′UTRs. At each anchor, an 8mer was assigned first; remaining sites could be 7mer-m8 or 7mer-A1. An 8mer pairs strand positions 2–8 plus target A opposite position 1; 7mer-m8 pairs positions 2–8; 7mer-A1 pairs positions 2–7 plus that A. Separate overlapping anchors and separate transcript isoforms remain distinct occurrences. Site, transcript and gene counts are different units.

The terminal-four-base-pair stability calculation is an unmodified-RNA heuristic at 37 °C and 1 M NaCl, using the declared nearest-neighbour convention. It compares paired ends, excludes overhangs and does not measure strand loading or gate eligibility, evaluation or selection.

Coverage limits

Variants and muscle expression remain unassessed. The searches do not cover genome/pre-mRNA/intronic sites, six-mer or noncanonical interactions, interactions outside the substitution bound, target accessibility, tissue exposure, biological suppression or safety. No other annotated coding isoform was available for the specified isoform-coverage assessment. A bounded zero count is not evidence of absence outside the search.
